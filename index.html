Flatball Connect

Everything for ultimate, in one app.

The rules, drills, strategies, coaching videos and pickup games near you. Free, with no ads.

  • The rulesThe WFDF Rules of Ultimate, quick answers for common calls, and the 24 hand signals.
  • A daily quizFive rules questions a day that get harder as your streak grows.
  • DrillsA drill library, and warm-ups to follow along.
  • StrategiesStacks, cuts, zones and marks, acted out as animated plays.
  • WatchCoaching videos and highlights, updated every day.
  • Find a gameA map of pickup games: post one, see who's going.
