Privacy policy

Flatball Connect · Effective 11 October 2026

Flatball Connect is an app for ultimate players, made by Bala Malaivendan (“we”). This says what the app collects, why, who else is involved, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You can use the rules, quiz, drills, strategies and videos without an account.
  • An account is needed only to post or join games, to message other players, and to keep your saved things across phones.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account, and what's linked to it, at any time from inside the app.

Without an account

What you save (drills, playlists, quiz answers and progress, settings) stays on your phone. We don't receive it.

What we collect, and why

Email address

To create your account and send sign-in and password-reset codes.

Only us
Username, display name, and male or female matching (MMP/FMP)

To identify you in games, and to count MMP and FMP players for mixed games.

Signed-in players
Profile photo (optional)

Shown beside your name in games.

Signed-in players
What you save in the app

Saved drills and lists, playlists, quiz answers and streak, progress and your teams, to keep them in step across your phones.

Only you
Games you post

Title, place, time, description, and your display name as organizer, so other players can find and join the game.

Anyone using the app
Games you join

Whether you're going, interested, or can't make it, so the organizer and players know who's coming.

Signed-in players
Messages

What you write in a game's chat or to another player, and when, so it reaches them. A game's chat is read by everyone in that game, including players who called out; a conversation with another player only by the two of you. The game's own notices (who joined, a new time) are part of its chat. In a chat with anyone under 18, a message is checked against a list of unsuitable words before it's sent.

The people in the chat
Your age group

Whether you're under 18 and, if you are, the month and year you turn 18, never your birthday. To keep messages between individual players to those 18 and over, so younger players can't be contacted privately by adults. Never shown to other players.

Only us
Finding you by name

Signed-in players can search for your username or display name to send you a message request, which you can accept or decline. Turn it off in Settings → Preferences; players in your games can still message you.

Signed-in players
Blocks and reports

Who you've blocked, so they can't message you; they aren't told. If you report a message, a copy of it and who sent it, so we can look into it; we get an email when one arrives, and the sender isn't told who reported it.

Only us
Bug reports and feature requests

Your message, an optional screenshot, the app version and your phone's system version, and, if you're signed out, an email for a reply if you give one. To fix problems and plan the app; we get an email when one arrives.

Only us
Failed sign-in attempts

A username and a time, to slow down password guessing. Deleted after a day.

Only us

Your location

If you allow it, Find a Game uses your phone's location to show games near you. It's sent with that search and not stored. You can say no and search by place instead. The places you pick when you post a game are stored with the game, as above.

Photos

The app reads a photo from your library only when you choose one, for your profile or a bug report.

Notifications

Game reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't send them, and no notification data leaves your phone.

Who else is involved

We use a few services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs, and your phone's internet address, as any website would.

Supabase

Stores accounts, saved data, games, messages and reports.

Resend

Sends the sign-in and reset emails, and our alerts for bug reports and reported messages.

YouTube

Plays the videos in Watch, through its official player, under Google's privacy policy.

OpenFreeMap and Photon (by Komoot)

The map and the place search. They receive the area you're looking at and what you type into the search.

GitHub

Hosts the daily video list, quiz updates and, on Android, the check for a new test build.

Google Maps

Opens only if you tap Directions, in Google's own app or website.

Donations are made in your own UPI app, never in Flatball Connect. We never see your bank details, only what our bank shows us about a payment (such as the amount and the payer's name). The Support page reports donations only as monthly totals, never with a name.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete your account.
  • Bug reports and feature requests: until we've dealt with them. If you delete your account, they're kept without any link to you.
  • Messages to another player: 90 days from when they're sent, then deleted.
  • A game's chat: deleted 30 days after the game.
  • Reports of messages: until we've dealt with them.
  • Failed sign-in attempts: one day.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → tap your profile → Delete account. This deletes at once your sign-in and email, your profile and photo, everything you've saved, games you organized and their chats, your place in other games, and your conversations with other players. What you wrote in other players' game chats stays, shown as “Deleted player”, until that chat is deleted 30 days after the game.

You can also ask us to delete it without the app.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in the app (Settings → tap your profile), and delete your account at any time. You can also write to us to ask what we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to raise a complaint, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the law where you live. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

You need to be at least 13 to create an account. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian first. To protect younger players from being contacted privately by adults, players under 18 can chat in their games' chats, but can't message other players one to one, receive their requests, or be found by name, until the month after they turn 18. In a game's chat with anyone under 18, messages with unsuitable words aren't sent. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your email and saved data can be read only by you and by us, your profile only by signed-in players, and a conversation only by the people in it, enforced by the database itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For a change that matters, we'll say so in the app's version notes.
